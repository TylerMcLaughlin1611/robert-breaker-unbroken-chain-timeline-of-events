01

Summary

On December 22, 2025, Robert Breaker released The Unbroken Chain of Salvation by Faith in The Blood, a book that says it documents twenty centuries of preachers teaching salvation by faith in the blood of Christ.

Many of the book’s quotations were generated by an AI chatbot rather than taken from the historical sources. By Breaker’s and Robert Potthoff’s own descriptions, an AI was asked to produce “verbatim quotes.” In my audit, only 3 of Chapter 8’s 78 quotes are genuine; 44 are AI-generated and 31 could not be verified. In Chapter 9, 87 of 91 quotes appear to be AI-generated and 4 could not be verified.

On February 16, 2026, Breaker said on a livestream that he had unpublished the book and wanted to fix it. The physical editions were pulled, but the English and Spanish digital editions stayed on sale. The Kindle edition I read on October 2, 2026 had not been revised.

My speculation is marked when it appears, I.E why Robert Breaker hasn't removed it.

The Amazon description calls the book “thoroughly documented” and says it draws on “modern A.I. search engine analysis.” It also promises to show “why A.I. itself confesses” that faith in the blood is necessary to be saved.

So the book advertised its use of AI from day one. What the listing did not say is that AI was also used to produce quotations attributed to historical figures.

Timeline

December 2025

22Dec 2025

The book is released.

23Dec 2025

“My New Book is Now Out!”

Breaker says he gives “many, many, many quotes” from well-known preachers and from Independent Baptist preachers. He encourages viewers to take the book to their pastors to see “whether or not they are preaching the true message of salvation.” Whatever one thinks of that goal, it made the book’s accuracy a practical matter: readers were being asked to measure their pastors against it.

26Dec 2025

“Do All Baptists Preach Faith in the Blood?”

Breaker says that asking Jesus into your heart or praying for salvation comes from Unitarianism and Universalism. Two things are worth checking.

Church historian Thomas Kidd, in “‘Ask Jesus into Your Heart’: A History of the Sinner’s Prayer” (The Gospel Coalition), traces that language to Puritan and evangelical roots, not Unitarian or Universalist ones. I checked his research and it holds up.

Early Christian writers describe asking and praying to God for salvation. One example:

“Jonah proclaimed destruction to the Ninevites; but they, repenting of their sins, propitiated God by prayer, and obtained salvation, although they were aliens of God.”

Clement of Rome, 1 Clement 7, c. 96–97 A.D.
27Dec 2025

“Will You Have Any Rewards in Heaven?”

Breaker ties heavenly rewards to his view of salvation and repeatedly tells viewers to buy the book.

28Dec 2025

“Faith in the Blood has been Preached for 2000 Years Now!!!”

Breaker holds up a proof copy, says he has ordered 10 copies, and reports that the book has sold 325 copies, adding that this matches Romans 3:25. This matters later: it shows he checks his sales numbers. Viewers, he says, are giving the book to relatives and to pastors.

That same day he posted “Is This the Worst Gospel Tract Ever?” which also links the book in its description.

30Dec 2025

“Are you Deceived by Another Gospel?”

Breaker reads from notes in an 1800s family Bible that a viewer sent him. I can’t find those notes online, and there is no way to verify what the Bible says beyond Breaker’s word. (I discuss this in Part 4 of my series Robert Breaker’s Salvation Theology Is a Modern Doctrine.)

January 2026

4Jan 2026

“Should Have Added This To My New Book!!!”

Breaker sings two hymns about the blood as further historical support.

15Jan 2026

“Saved by Your Prayer?”

Breaker presents 52 quotes from historical figures said to deny the sinner’s prayer. As far as I can tell, only one of the 52 is real, and it has been modified; the other 51 trace back only to Breaker. They share a pattern: deny the sinner’s prayer, affirm the blood. Meaning, they are also likely AI-generated quotes.

15Jan 2026

“Why I Wrote This Book!”

This is the most substantive of the promotional videos. Breaker says he traveled to libraries and Bible schools across America looking for quotes from old preachers. He also says his mother read the book and told him, “I don’t think you wrote that book. I think God wrote that book.” He replied that the Holy Spirit helped, but he wrote it.

He then reads quotes from Chapter 8. Three are worth comparing with the real sources.

Gregory the Great, c. 600
As quoted in the book
Not the ceremonies of man, but faith in His sacrifice. AI-generated
 
The record

Gregory was a pope, which is an odd witness for a Baptist argument, and this quotation is also AI-generated.

John Wesley
As quoted in the book
By faith in His blood. Through faith in His blood, here lies the way to justification: not by merit nor penance, but by a believing trust in the blood of Jesus Christ. Another AI-generated quote
The Means of Grace
This is real Wesley
all who desire the grace of God are to wait for it in the way of prayer… Ask, and it shall be given you.
John Chrysostom, c. 400
As quoted in the book
The blood has done everything… Let us then come boldly, not trusting ourselves, but trusting the blood. Another AI-generated quote
Homily 10 on Acts
What Chrysostom actually says
Call upon Him for the benefit of your enemy; call upon Him for the salvation of your own soul; then He will be present.

In the same video Breaker says, “I never found any quote of them saying, ‘Just ask Jesus into your heart,’ or ‘Ask God to save you.’”

While he said he had quotes from historical figures, only 3 of the entire 78 quotes were real, and those were on the Eucharist. And of those quotes, he said that the AI was implicitly mistaken about the "blood in the cup" (his wording for the Eucharist) and the blood on the cross (his theology). So those aren't applicable.

18Jan 2026

Tony publishes a warning

“Robert Breaker book: The Unbroken Chain of Salvation by Faith in the Blood: WARNING!” goes through part of Chapter 8.

24Jan 2026

“Never Stop Preaching Faith in the Blood!!!”

In this video, which links the book in its description, Breaker responds to critics: “I’m not making these up… These books are out there. You can find them.” He says he has 35 pages of Dr. Ruckman quotes about the blood on his RRB3 site. I couldn’t find 35 pages. What I did find is a page quoting Ruckman’s The Simplicity of Salvation (1987).

However, at the end of the book, Dr. Ruckman says this: “If you don’t know that, it’s because you are lost.” The same book ends with an invitation to pray:

“Wherever you are, bow your head and pray a simple prayer something like this: ‘Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner, and unless you save me, I am lost forever. I now come to you, the best way I know how, and ask you to save me.’”

Peter S. Ruckman, The Simplicity of Salvation (1987)

See the quote mining.

27Jan 2026

“Robert Breaker’s new Book, Chapter 8”

Tony publishes a video that goes through a spreadsheet of every Chapter 8 quote and finds all but three of the 78 to be fake. For transparency, I had to correct him on John Wesley. I have since checked Chapter 8 quote by quote myself: see my Chapter 8 audit.

Note on Wesley: Tony’s notes on Wesley conflate Wesley’s view of confession in Romans 10 with petitionary salvation. Wesley did teach that people should ask God for grace as a means of grace, but he did not harmonize that with his comments on Romans 10.

February 2026

16Feb 2026

The livestream Turning point

Breaker and Robert Potthoff, a Breaker supporter, hold a livestream titled “Learning About A.I. (How to Use It).” The main points:

  • The accounts of who used the AI conflict. Breaker says he was overextended, that he “went to brother Potthoff” to fill in missing material, and that Potthoff “went to AI.” Potthoff says he prompted the AI to produce verbatim quotes and then twice asked it to verify them. By June 2 Breaker is saying, “I went to AI and asked for some truths.” The livestream and the June video can’t both be the whole story.
  • “The spirit of the quote.” Potthoff says the AI sometimes “gave me the spirit of the quote instead of the actual quote,” because some sources were in Latin or Greek and were translated. A quotation in quotation marks is a claim that those are the words in the source. A language model can’t supply the spirit of a text it hasn’t read.
  • Potthoff says the only method he would recommend is using agent mode, one person at a time, with the AI showing where it found each quote. Neither man says that was done for Chapter 8, which suggests it was ordinary prompting (I.E not using RAG/searching the internet with an LLM).
  • What Breaker admitted. He said he trusted the AI, that “some of them are paraphrases without a doubt and some may even be made up,” and that he wasn’t comfortable with that doubt. He told listeners they could ask Amazon for refunds, said he had unpublished the book, and said he would rewrite it.
  • He also tried to explain some of the quotes by saying AI had pulled Roman Catholic statements about the blood in the sacrament, “the wrong blood,” and leaned on Augustine, whose wider soteriology is not a Baptist one. That is only applicable to the three out of context quotes, the remaining 75 are just fake.
  • The physical English and Spanish editions were pulled. The digital English and Spanish editions were not. I can’t say why. It may be oversight, HOWEVER Breaker has said he follows his sales figures. As of October 2, 2026, the Kindle edition was still on sale.

March to June 2026

1Mar 2026

“True Baptists Preach Faith in the Blood!!!”

Breaker reads commentaries I cover in Parts 2 and 3 of my series against his livestream "Faith in the Blood is not a modern doctrine". One is from Hyde Park Publishers, whose church explicitly supports the sinner’s prayer and tells readers to pray one to be saved. At the end he holds up a physical copy of The Unbroken Chain and says anyone not preaching the blood atonement is “preaching another gospel.”

2Jun 2026

“AI or ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE Walkin and A-Talking”

Breaker describes the Chapter 8 episode in the first person. “So we went to AI and we asked for quotes on faith in the blood,” he says, then: “I went to AI and asked for some truths and it says here are verbatim historically documented quotes.” He adds that “AI gave me some paraphrases and didn’t give me the actual quotes.” He also says he uses AI to make thumbnails, and he complains in the same video that AI is bad for research. This, of course is contradicting the idea that Potthoff was the one who generated the AI quotes.

“DON’T LET THE DEVIL WIN! Walkin and A-Talking”

The thumbnail appears to be AI-generated: Breaker looks on while a devil figure is trampled by a foot belonging to Jesus. The devil resembles a television fisherman (and far more popular) who shares my name, Tyler McLaughlin of Wicked Tuna. Breaker said on June 2 that he uses AI for thumbnails.

Observation 01

None of these reviews mentions the quote controversy, and the ratings stayed high afterward. The livestream did not seem to reach many buyers.

Observation 02

Several "paperback" reviews appear after the physical edition was pulled. They may be because of earlier purchases or third-party sellers (Breaker said third parties could still sell it), and the July 18 reviewer’s request for a paperback fits the physical edition being unavailable.

However, the July 18 Kindle review means Robert Breaker is still getting money for it.

04

Current happenings

On October 2, 2026 I subscribed to Kindle Unlimited to read the current edition. It has not been updated. Chapters 8 and 9 are unchanged, and I found no correction or notice.

Even conceding that he didn't know right from wrong when using AI, he never actually pulled the digital versions in either English or Spanish. The online versions are still getting sales, and he clearly admitted to having seen the Amazon book sales dashboard earlier, so he is aware that the book is getting sales. Yet he is still doing nothing, despite A. promising to pull the book and B. not actually giving refunds!
